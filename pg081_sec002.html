Soma shairi na habari, kisha jibu maswali yanayofuata.
Shairi
Tuyatunze mazingira
1. Leo tumekusanyika, mazingira kuyajua,
Mama baba nawe kaka, ni vema mkasogea,
Ili nipate sikika, kisha nanyi tachangia,
Sipotunza mazingira, nayo hayatatutunza.
2. Tukitunza mazingira, faida tajipatia,
Tutaepuka madhara, kamwe maradhi sikia,
Tutapata afya bora, hewa safi nakwambia,
Sipotunza mazingira, nayo hayatatutunza.
3. Miti hutusaidia, kwa mbao matunda pia,
Kivuli hutupatia, mvua kutuletea,
Mmomonyoko zuia, ardhi itatulia,
Sipotunza mazingira, nayo hayatatutunza.
